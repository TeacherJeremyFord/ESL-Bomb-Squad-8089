# M12: Animal Race Day

**Target vocab (25):** turtle, dog, cat, monkey, rabbit, frog, bird, fish, lion, bear, tiger, pig, cow, horse, swim, run, jump, jog, fly, walk, can, not, zoo, farm, other
**Off-list words needed:** race, team, gate, press, now, first
**Difficulty:** A1–B1 · **Time:** 60–90 s · **Route:** puzzle-first (timed reaction), then words fitted to it
**Status:** Draft

## The hook

It's race day for the **Zoo team** and the **Farm team**. Animals parade past the starting
gate, one by one. Each race needs the right animal from the right team, and the Defuser has to
press **GO** while that animal is in the gate. The Expert has the entry list. Nobody has time
to think slowly.

## Inspiration

Timed-reaction modules, where something passes and you have to act at the right moment,
combined with shouted-instruction pressure (the Spaceteam family). It's the "panic module" of
the set: short phrases, fast listening, and quick recognition of animal and action words.

## Defuser view

- **Event board:** an **action icon** for the current race. Swim (waves), run (speed
  lines), jump (hurdle), jog (sweatband, easy pace), fly (wings), walk (footprints). Plus a
  **team flag**: ZOO (lion flag) or FARM (barn flag).
- **The parade:** animals walk past a gate, one every ~1.5 s. All 14 animals are possible
  (turtle, dog, cat, monkey, rabbit, frog, bird, fish in a bowl, lion, bear, tiger, pig,
  cow, horse). Each animal wears a **team shirt**, and the same animal can appear on both
  teams.
- A **NOT light** (red ✖) that sometimes lights up for a race.
- A big **GO** button.
- The race counter shows **race 1 of 3**.

## Expert manual

The Expert's screen shows today's **entry list** (new every round). Animals are **not**
entered for what they're good at. This is a silly race day.

| Race | Zoo team | Farm team |
|---|---|---|
| swim | tiger (other: monkey) | horse (other: pig) |
| run | turtle (other: bear) | cow (other: dog) |
| jump | fish (other: lion) | pig (other: rabbit) |
| jog | bird (other: tiger) | cat (other: frog) |
| fly | bear (other: lion) | frog (other: horse) |
| walk | monkey (other: bird) | rabbit (other: cow) |

> **Rules:**
> 1. Ask: *What is the race? What is the team?*
> 2. Find the animal. Tell the Defuser: *"The tiger! Zoo team!"*
> 3. Press GO when that animal is in the gate.
> 4. If the NOT light is on, the first animal can **not** race. Press GO for the **other**
>    animal.

## Solving it

- The Defuser calls the event: *"Swim! Zoo!"* The Expert finds *"Tiger!"*, and the Defuser
  watches the parade.
- **Team shirts matter.** A FARM tiger walking past first is a classic trap.
- **The NOT light** forces a second lookup under pressure: *"Not tiger! The other one…
  monkey!"*
- Three races per bomb. Pressing GO on the wrong animal, or letting the right one pass twice
  (it comes back once), is a strike.

**Real-world knowledge is a trap here.** The bird jogs and the fish jumps, so a Defuser who
guesses "fly = bird" gets it wrong. That keeps the information gap airtight. It's also funny,
and that's on purpose.

## Example round

> **D:** Race one! Waves… swim! Farm!
> **E:** Swim, farm… horse!
> **D:** Dog… cat… pig… HORSE! Is it farm? Yes! *(GO)*
> **D:** Race two! Wings… fly. Zoo. And red light!
> **E:** Fly, zoo: bear. Red light… not bear. The other: lion!
> **D:** Lion… zoo shirt? No, farm. … Lion, zoo! *(GO)*

## Information gap

✅ **Hidden entry list that contradicts common sense + a conditional (NOT light).** The
Defuser can't guess, and the Expert can't see the parade.

## Difficulty scaling

- **A1:** No teams (one shirt color), no NOT light, slow parade (2.5 s), 6 animals.
- **A2:** Teams on, 10 animals, 2 s parade.
- **B1:** All 14 animals, NOT light on, 1.5 s parade, decoy animals of the same kind on the
  other team.

## Build notes

- Parade generation: guarantee that the target animal (on the right team) appears within the
  first ~8 passes, with at least one same-animal decoy before it at B1.
- Speed must be tunable per class. A teacher slider matters here more than in any other
  module.
- The verb icons (walk / jog / run) are the riskiest art. Get them distinct before anything
  else, or the module is unfair.
